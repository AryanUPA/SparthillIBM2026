About CivicSignal

Useful context. Your call.

A prototype for helping residents move from “I noticed something” to a clearer, source-linked next step.

What the assistant does

Local demo rules draft a summary, suggest a category, and match the description against a small sample directory. The suggestion is not an official decision.

What it does not do

It does not submit reports, contact agencies, verify live service availability, or guarantee a response. You review and decide what to do.

Your data in this prototype

No account, analytics, or backend storage. Your issue stays in browser memory for this session. Don’t enter contact details or other sensitive information.

Demo directory sources

The routes are examples based on public information from these agencies. They are not tailored to your location; confirm local applicability before using them.
